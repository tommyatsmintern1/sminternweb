Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information on the SM Intern site.

Information we collect

We collect the information you provide when you register, log in, or save profile details. This may include your name, email address, CV content, and any files you upload.

How we use your data

We use your information to manage your account, provide access to internship positions, and improve the service. Your email is used for authentication and account-related communication only.

Data storage and security

We store account and profile data securely using the configured backend services. We follow standard security practices such as encrypted authentication and restricted access to personally identifiable data.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data when required by law or to operate the service as described in this policy.

Cookies and analytics

We may use basic cookies or browser storage for authentication and to maintain your session. No third-party tracking is used beyond the platform services needed for login and site performance.

Data deletion

If you choose to delete your account, your profile and uploaded data will be removed from our system in accordance with the service's data retention policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please email hello@smintern.co.uk.
