Trust & safety

How we verify businesses

Every business earns a verification badge before going live. Here's exactly what we check:

Step 1
🏛️
Companies House

We search your business at the official Companies House registry to confirm you're a registered UK company. No real registration — no listing.

Step 2
🌐
Online presence

We verify you have a working website or active social media. This instantly filters out fake or ghost listings before any student sees them.

Step 3
📧
Email confirmation

We send a confirmation from hello@smintern.co.uk to verify your email is real and your listing is intentional. Simple and effective.

SM Intern Verified Once verified, your listing displays this badge — building instant trust with students.
