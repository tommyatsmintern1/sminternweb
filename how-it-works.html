How we verify businesses

Every business gets a verification badge. Here's our process:

Step 1 — Companies House check

We search your business at find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk to confirm you're a registered UK company.

Step 2 — Online presence

We verify you have a working website or active social media. This filters out fake listings instantly.

Step 3 — Email confirmation

We send a confirmation email from hello@smintern.co.uk to confirm your listing is live and verify your email is real.

Once verified, your listing displays the SM Intern verification badge.
