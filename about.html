Our story

Built at Exeter.
Built for students.

SM Intern was founded to solve a problem I saw firsthand — students hungry for real experience, and small businesses that needed motivated talent but couldn't access big graduate schemes.

Founder

Hey, I'm Tommy.

I'm an engineering student at the University of Exeter. While studying, I noticed a gap — local small businesses were struggling to find short-term project help, and students were stuck applying to the same saturated grad schemes.

SM Intern is my answer to that. A platform that connects verified, growth-focused SMEs with students who want real projects, not coffee runs.

The focus is on meaningful work: clear deliverables, proper onboarding, and outcomes that genuinely matter to both sides.
